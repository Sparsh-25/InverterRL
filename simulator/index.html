Inverter Battery Simulator

Each hour the battery is charged, held or used, and power cuts arrive with the probabilities measured at Guddiangadi, Karnataka (ESMI 2018). All three lanes face the same cuts, so differences come from the policies alone.

Score = −(bill) − κ × (blackout hours). Regret is how much worse than the DP optimum the policy scores, in % of the optimum, on the same cuts. A few days are noisy; run 1,000 days to compare with Tables X and XI of the report.

The policies are the project's real results: DP optimal policies from value iteration, the hand-written rule, and the trained SARSA, Q-learning, Monte Carlo and DQN policies from results/ (the seed with the median final regret). “Then summer” uses the policies trained 50,000 episodes on the normal pattern and 50,000 on summer with ε reset to 0.5; they exist only for κ = 50.

Each run starts at midnight with the battery at 5 units and the grid on. The simulation follows env.py step by step: one unit of demand per hour, Charge buys 2 units, Hold buys 1, Discharge buys none, and during a cut the battery runs the house whatever the action.
